Account & Data Deletion

Request the permanent deletion of your lPortfolio registered account and all associated personal data.

📱 Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the lPortfolio app installed on your device, you can delete your account instantly without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Open the lPortfolio app on your Android device.
  2. Tap on Settings in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Account Management (or tap your profile header).
  4. Tap Delete Account at the bottom and confirm the dialog prompt.

* In-app deletion executes immediately and purges your authentication tokens and database records in real-time.

🌐 Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

Important Notice: Account deletion is irreversible. Once processed, your user profile, authentication credentials, synchronized preferences, and cloud-stored data will be permanently wiped.
