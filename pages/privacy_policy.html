2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

a. Account and Authentication Data

You may browse the core portfolio and showcase features without creating an account. If you register or log in to sync settings or access interactive features, we collect your email address, username, and encrypted password hash. We use this strictly to authenticate your identity, prevent unauthorized access, and manage your account.

b. AI Assistant (Gemini / Firebase AI)

The Application features an AI Intro Assistant powered by Google Gemini (gemini-3.7-flash via the official firebase_ai SDK). When you chat with the AI assistant:

  • Your query text is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to Google's AI backend solely to generate relevant, interactive responses about the developer's experience, architecture, and projects.
  • Your prompts are NOT used to build personal advertising profiles or sold to data brokers.
  • We integrate Firebase App Check (Play Integrity on Android) to verify that requests originate from legitimate app binaries and prevent unauthorized API abuse.

c. Local Storage and Preferences

We use local storage (SharedPreferences / FlutterSecureStorage) to remember your local preferences on your device, including:

  • Selected Theme Mode (Light / Dark / System Follow)
  • Custom Font Family and Dynamic Font Size scaling
  • Language preferences (English / Japanese / Chinese)
  • Security tokens (Stored in encrypted hardware-backed storage)

This data stays locally on your device unless you choose to back it up or sync it with your registered account.

d. Device Permissions

The Application requests only minimal, necessary system permissions:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to fetch portfolio data, communicate with the API, and interact with the AI assistant.
  • Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional permission requested on Android 13+ to notify you about major app updates, featured technical articles, or interactive responses.

We do NOT request access to sensitive hardware permissions such as Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, or external Photo/File Storage.

4. Data Security and Encryption

We implement robust industry-standard technical measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile app, our backend servers, and Google APIs are encrypted using HTTPS / TLS 1.3.
  • Encryption at Rest: Authentication tokens stored on the client use Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256). Passwords on our servers are securely hashed using salted cryptographic hashing algorithms.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertising networks under any circumstances.

5. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In full compliance with Google Play's App Data Deletion Policy, users have the complete right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and associated data through either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion (Instant): Open the lPortfolio App → Navigate to Settings → Select Account → Tap Delete Account. Your credentials and synced profile are immediately deleted from active servers.
  2. Web Deletion Request Form: Visit our dedicated Web Account Deletion Portal to submit an external deletion request.
  3. Direct Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to listen2code@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Deletion SLA: In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Web and email requests are verified and processed within 30 days. All user profile records, email associations, and cloud preferences are permanently purged from our active databases and backup logs.
